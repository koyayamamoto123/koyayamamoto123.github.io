About

🧑‍🚀

I'm an aerospace engineer focused on guidance, navigation, and control of spacecraft and autonomous aerial systems. My work bridges rigorous dynamics modeling with practical, flight-ready software.

Currently at Texas A&M University, I study how vehicles sense their environment and make robust decisions under uncertainty — from orbital rendezvous to atmospheric flight.

When I'm not deriving equations of motion, you'll find me building, testing, and occasionally launching things that shouldn't come back down too fast.

Research

My research spans the control, estimation, and dynamics of aerospace vehicles. Here are the main areas I work on.

🛰️

Spacecraft GNC

Guidance, navigation, and control for orbital maneuvers, rendezvous, and proximity operations.

📡

State Estimation

Kalman filtering and sensor fusion for robust navigation under noisy, uncertain conditions.

🎯

Optimal Control

Trajectory optimization and model-predictive control for fuel-efficient, constraint-aware flight.
